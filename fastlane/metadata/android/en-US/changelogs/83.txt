Koler 1.7.0 is a full rewrite with a new interface.

- New look: contact photos everywhere, one floating bar, landscape call screen
- Answer, decline and hang up from the call notification
- Koler shows its own missed-call notifications
- Screen stays on during speaker calls
- Call screen respects Do Not Disturb allowed callers
- Add call, merge and conference calls fixed
- Block numbers and open WhatsApp from a contact
- Themed (monochrome) app icon
- Translations for 14 languages
